# PFS3 engineering design and provenance

The authoritative on-disk format, algorithms, feature gates, recovery semantics and verification requirements are integrated into the [unified technical reference](../../../docs/PFS3-TECHNICAL-REFERENCE.md) and [installed PDF](../../../docs/PFS3.pdf). This supersedes the duplicated format chapters in the earlier design document.

The [former design and source-layout record](https://github.com/Infiltrator-Projects/Filesystem-PFS3/blob/ea09be0924e38d9f9592df334c27e5d97f2918ab/native/filesystems/pfs3/DESIGN.md) is preserved at its exact historical revision for attribution, earlier adapter layouts and migration evidence. Earlier Linux/Windows adapter status is not a production capability claim. Current provider status is defined by the [README](../../../README.md), exported Common V2 operation masks and exact-revision qualification. The engine, not the host or consumer, owns filesystem semantics. Reject malformed media and unqualified features; do not infer write, recovery or firmware support from format knowledge or a successful compile.
